Support

Need help? Email dannielappdevelopment@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Which devices does QuoteWalk work on?

Room scanning needs a device with a LiDAR scanner: iPhone Pro and Pro Max models (iPhone 12 Pro or later) and iPad Pro models with LiDAR.

How accurate are the measurements?

LiDAR measurements are very good but not perfect. Always check key measurements before you order materials or confirm a price. You can edit any area in the app.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open QuoteWalk and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, or go to Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In QuoteWalk go to Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription. You can also do this on your iPhone in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. QuoteWalk has no account and no servers, so we can't recover data from a lost or reset device. Keep your iPhone backed up with iCloud Backup or to a computer.
