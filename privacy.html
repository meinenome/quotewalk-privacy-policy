Privacy Policy

QuoteWalk: Room Scan Quotes · Effective 22 October 2026

In short
  • We don't collect, store or sell any of your data. We have no servers.
  • Your business details, customers, room scans, quotes and signatures are stored only on your device.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple.

This policy explains how the QuoteWalk iPhone and iPad app ("QuoteWalk", "the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the app and to this website.

Information we collect

None. QuoteWalk does not collect, receive or have access to any personal information or usage data. The app has no account sign-up, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following information only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your business details (such as business name, contact details, tax settings, rates and quote settings).
  • Customer details you enter (such as name, phone, email and site address).
  • Room scans, measurements, quotes and quote lines.
  • Customer signatures captured when a quote is signed on your device.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is not synced to iCloud by QuoteWalk. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on, under Apple's own terms.

You are responsible for the customer information you choose to enter. Please only collect what you need to prepare and send a quote.

Camera and LiDAR

QuoteWalk uses your device's camera and LiDAR scanner, through Apple's RoomPlan framework, to measure rooms. Scanning is processed on your device. No photos or video are recorded or uploaded. Only the resulting room measurements and 3D room outline are saved on your device. You can turn off camera access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, but room scanning won't work without it.

Sharing quotes

A quote only leaves your device when you choose to share it, for example by sending the PDF through Messages, Mail or another app using the iOS share sheet, or by copying a summary. What happens to it after that is governed by the app or service you choose and the person you send it to.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit. That information comes from Apple and is not sent to us. Apple's handling of your data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Crash reports and App Store statistics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. These do not identify you. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual quotes in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device (apart from any copies in your own backups or in quotes you have already shared). Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf.

Children

QuoteWalk is a business tool for tradespeople and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Act, the EU and UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete personal information held about you. Because QuoteWalk does not collect or hold any personal information, we have none to provide or delete. We do not sell or share personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change means the app starts handling data differently, we'll also explain it in the app before it takes effect.
